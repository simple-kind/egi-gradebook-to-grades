How a final letter grade is computed
  1. For each student, pull their LT 1 through LT 7 summary-assignment grades (each a 0–4 score).
  2. Any LT that's blank or marked exempt is dropped entirely — it doesn't count toward the total, it's not treated as a zero.
  3. From the remaining (graded) LTs, compute two fractions:
    • pct_2 = fraction of graded LTs scoring ≥ 2
    • pct_3 = fraction of graded LTs scoring ≥ 3
  4. Apply thresholds, in order:
    • A: pct_2 == 1 and pct_3 ≥ .66 (every graded LT at least a 2, two-thirds at least a 3)
    • B: pct_2 == 1 and pct_3 ≥ .5
    • C: pct_2 ≥ .66
    • D: pct_2 ≥ .5
    • F: otherwise
  5. If every LT is exempt/blank, the student gets no grade (N/A) instead of an F.

No individual LT is weighted more than another — the whole formula is driven by how many LTs clear the 2 and 3 bars, not a weighted average.
